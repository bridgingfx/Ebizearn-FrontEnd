## 1. Summary

Today's work focused on five areas:

1. **Access control.** Super Admin can now control platform access sidebar section by sidebar section for every role (Admin, Moderator, Business, Contributor). Super Admin can also assign specific users to individual admins and moderators. Permission changes now reach users' sidebars within about a minute, without signing in again.
2. **Business team accounts.** A business owner can now add team members, choose what each member can access, and see who did what.
3. **Campaign post content.** Each campaign can provide its post text in Manual mode (one approved text) or Auto mode (every contributor gets their own AI-reworded version). Staff approve the content before contributors can see the tasks, and all content is checked for offensive or inappropriate language.
4. **Contributor levels.** Contributors now move up a level based on completed tasks, using the thresholds admin sets. Admin can also set and lock a contributor's level by hand.
5. **Production fixes.** Task visibility, deployment, session handling, country-change compliance, and mobile layout were fixed.

All changes are committed to the `main` branch of both repositories. The frontend deploys automatically. The backend requires the deployment steps in Section 5.

## 2. Features Delivered

### 2.1 Roles & Permissions by sidebar section

- Every admin sidebar page has its own permission:
  - Users, Businesses, KYC, Social Channels
  - Deposits, Withdrawals, Wallets
  - Fraud & Risk, Referrals, Demo Requests
  - Analytics, Website Traffic, System Health, Audit Logs
  - and the remaining pages.
- Super Admin can switch each page on or off per role or per individual account. The sidebar shows only the pages a person is allowed to use, and the API enforces the same rules.
- Platforms, Payment Gateways, Email & Campaigns and Contributor Ranks remain Super Admin only.
- **Controlled delegation:**
  - An admin can grant access to the users below them only from the permissions the admin holds.
  - A moderator can do the same for their own contributors and businesses.
  - Neither can change the admin role or other staff accounts.
- **No disruption on release:** existing grants were copied into the new permissions, so nobody gains or loses access when this goes live.

### 2.2 Sidebars for every role follow permissions, with live updates

- The sidebars for all four panels (Admin, Moderator, Business, Contributor) now show only the sections the person is allowed to use. This applies on desktop and on the mobile menu.
- The Contributor sidebar follows the same rules:
  - Tasks and My Tasks — task permission.
  - Wallet — withdrawal permission.
  - Referrals — referral permission.
  - Support — support permission.
- **Live updates:** previously a change took effect only after the user signed in again. Now the user's sidebar updates within about a minute, or immediately when they return to the browser tab.
- Extra access granted to a single user (a per-user override) appears in that user's sidebar in the same way.

### 2.3 Assigning users to admins and moderators

- Super Admin can assign businesses, contributors and moderators to a specific admin. Businesses and contributors can be assigned to a moderator.
- An admin or moderator with assigned users sees and manages only those users and their data: KYC, withdrawals, deposits, wallets, support tickets, campaigns and tasks.
- The user list shows "Managed by …" for each assigned user.

### 2.4 Wallet adjustments

- A new **Add / remove wallet money** permission was added. Only Super Admin has it by default, and it can be granted to selected admins.
- Every manual credit or debit requires a reason and is recorded in the audit log.

### 2.5 Business Team Access

- **Adding members:** the business owner adds team members on the Team Access page (name, email, password).
- **Choosing access:** the owner selects which sections each member can use (Campaigns, Task Library, Proof Gallery, Analytics, Billing, Support).
- **Limits on access:**
  - A member can never have more access than the owner.
  - If Super Admin removes a permission from the owner, all of that owner's members lose it too.
- **Shared account:**
  - Members sign in through the normal business login page (`/business/login`). Forgot-password works for them as usual.
  - Members work inside the owner's business account, and all their work appears in the owner's account.
  - A member's email address cannot be used to register a new account.
- **Managing members:** the owner can suspend, reactivate or remove a member at any time. Members are blocked automatically if the owner's account is suspended.

### 2.6 Activity attribution ("created by")

- Campaigns, tasks and deposit requests now record which account created them.
- The business owner, Admin and Super Admin can see this as **"by {name} · Team member / Owner"**. This clearly separates the owner's actions from a team member's or staff member's.
- Proof reviews already recorded the reviewer's name.

### 2.7 Content safety for AI-generated campaign content

Contributors copy this content and post it publicly under their own names, so it must always be appropriate.

- **Copy-ready output only:** the AI returns just the post text. Introductions such as "Here is your post", quotation marks and formatting are removed automatically.
- **Strict content rules:** the AI is instructed to use clean, polite, family-friendly language. It must avoid:
  - swear words, even censored ones;
  - sexual, violent or hateful content;
  - discrimination of any kind;
  - politics and religion;
  - insults to competitors;
  - false or exaggerated claims.
- **Checked at every step:**
  1. The business's description is checked before the AI is called.
  2. The AI's output is checked before anyone sees it. If it fails, the AI retries once with stricter settings; inappropriate content is never shown.
  3. The text is checked again when the campaign is saved, including any manual edits by the business.
- **Two layers of detection:**
  - A maintained list of blocked words. It covers English, plus common Tamil and Hindi abuse written in English letters, and catches disguised spellings such as `sh1t` or `f*ck`.
  - OpenAI's moderation service, which judges context.
  - Everyday marketing phrases (for example "photo shoot", "kills 99% of germs") are deliberately allowed.
- The campaign wizard tells businesses that offensive or inappropriate words are blocked.

### 2.8 Campaign post content: Manual or Auto, approved by staff

- **Choice of mode:** when creating a campaign, the business chooses:
  - **No post text** — contributors follow the instructions only;
  - **Manual** — one text, written or generated by the business, that every contributor copies;
  - **Auto (AI)** — the business approves a sample, and each contributor who takes the task receives their own reworded version of it.
- **Why Auto matters:** when many people publish identical text, social platforms can flag the posts as spam. Auto-mode versions keep the same meaning, brand, links and hashtags, and add nothing new.
- **Admin control:** Admin / Super Admin can change the mode or rewrite the text for any campaign.
- **Approval before going live:**
  - Staff review the content on the campaign details page, then approve it or reject it with a note for the business.
  - The campaign's tasks stay hidden from contributors, and cannot be started, until the content is approved.
- **Delivered by the platform:** contributors receive their post text after they reserve the task, with a one-click copy button. If the AI service is unavailable, they receive the approved text instead.
- **Safety:** every version, whether manual, staff-edited or AI-generated, passes the content-safety checks in section 2.7.
- **Bug fixed along the way:** content written in the campaign wizard was never saved before. It is now saved and delivered correctly, including for draft campaigns.

### 2.9 Contributor levels based on completed tasks

- Contributors move up through the levels (Starter → Explorer → Trusted → Pro → Elite) automatically as their tasks are approved.
- Admin sets how many completed tasks each level needs on the **Contributor Ranks** page. Earnings are no longer required.
- If an approval is reversed, the level is recalculated and can go back down.
- **Manual control:** on a contributor's admin page, staff can set the level by hand and lock it. While it is locked, task completions do not change it; unlocking recalculates it straight away.
- **Bug fixed along the way:** two separate level systems were running, and one used fixed thresholds that ignored the admin's settings. There is now a single system that follows the admin's settings.

### 2.10 Country change compliance

- When a contributor changes their country of residence, the change is sent to staff for approval.
- After approval, the contributor must complete KYC again for the new country.

## 5. Deployment Steps (Backend)

The frontend deploys automatically from `main`. For the backend, in cPanel:

1. **Git Version Control** → **Update from Remote** → **Deploy HEAD Commit**.
2. The deployment script copies the files, installs dependencies, runs the database migrations and clears the caches automatically.

If the deploy button is blocked, open the cPanel Terminal, run `git status` in `~/repositories/Ebizearn-BackEnd`, and remove any server-generated file it lists (for example `error_log`).

New database changes in this release:

- Country change requests.
- Sidebar section permissions.
- Staff user assignments.
- Business team members and business section permissions.
- A "created by" field on campaigns, tasks and deposit requests.
- Campaign content mode and approval status, each contributor's own post text, and a level lock for contributors.
  - Existing campaigns that already had post text are kept live as approved Manual content.
  - Level thresholds now count completed tasks only.

## 6. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Review-platform compliance (important):**
   - The AI content generator currently supports Google Review and Trustpilot. Contributors post these reviews as their own in exchange for a reward.
   - Incentivised or ghost-written reviews break Google's and Trustpilot's policies. They can lead to reviews being removed, businesses being penalised, and contributor accounts being banned.
   - In several markets (for example the UK, the US and the EU) they may also break consumer protection law.
   - I recommend removing review platforms from AI generation, or changing them to "share your honest experience" tasks.

   - AI content generation;
   - content approval and Auto-mode versions;
   - contributor level changes.
